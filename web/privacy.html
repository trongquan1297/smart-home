Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what data MSmart collects when you use the MSmart app (iOS, Android), the MSmart home controller installed in your home, the camera add-on and the Google Home and Apple Home integrations; what we use that data for, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

Data controller: Newmoon22 Studios. Privacy contact: info@newmoon.cloud. In this policy, “MSmart”, “we” and “us” refer to Newmoon22 Studios.

1. Data we collect

2. How we use data

  • To provide the service: sign-in, showing and controlling your devices, running scenes, schedules and automation rules.
  • To send the alerts and notifications you enabled (sensors, automatic scenes, speaker announcements).
  • For access control: only people with access to a home can see and control its devices.
  • To switch scenes automatically when everyone has left home (if you enable it).
  • To sync with Google Home or Apple Home when you choose to link them.
  • To protect accounts and the system: preventing password guessing, detecting and fixing problems.

We do not sell personal data, do not use your data for advertising, and do not build advertising profiles about you.

3. Sharing with third parties

RecipientData and purpose

Within a home, people you invite see data according to their role: members see devices and the member list; renters see only rooms and devices, not the member list. We may disclose data to competent authorities when required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored on servers managed by us in Vietnam. Some data stays only on the controller in your home (such as camera passwords and the Zigbee network of your devices).

  • Alert history: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: expire over time and are deleted after expiry; signing out in the app revokes that device’s session.
  • Revoked Google Home links: kept for 30 more days for troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • “At home / away” status: deleted when the home location is changed or removed.
  • Account, homes, devices and control history: kept while you use the service; deleted when you delete the device or home, or delete your account.

5. Security

  • The app and the home controller connect to our servers over HTTPS / WSS (encrypted).
  • Passwords, invitation codes and access tokens are stored as hashes.
  • The home controller connects outbound; no ports need to be opened on your home router.
  • Every control command is permission-checked on the server; renters lose access as soon as their period ends or is revoked.
  • Failed sign-in attempts are rate-limited to prevent password guessing.

No system is completely secure. If an incident affects your data, we will notify you and handle it as required by law.

6. Your rights and choices

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct and request deletion of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to or request restriction of processing, and to lodge a complaint, in accordance with applicable personal data protection law.

Legal basis: [citation of the applicable personal data protection law].

  • Delete your account in the app: Homes screen → account icon → Delete account. Your account, sessions and Google Home link are deleted immediately; homes where you are the only administrator are deleted together with their devices; your Sign in with Apple link is revoked with Apple.
  • Also in the app: rename rooms and devices; delete scenes, rules, cameras and speakers; leave a home or revoke members; turn off “Switch scene when leaving home” and revoke location permission in your phone settings; turn off notifications; sign out.
  • Google Home: unlink at any time in the Google Home app.
  • Change your account name, sign out of all devices, access or receive a copy of your data: email info@newmoon.cloud from your account email. We will verify your request and respond within 2 days.

7. Children

MSmart is intended for people who manage a home. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Children in a family should use the app only when invited by, and with the consent of, a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes (for example a new data category or a new recipient), we will notify you in the app before they apply.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request, please email info@newmoon.cloud.
